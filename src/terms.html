1. What KaantaMandi does

KaantaMandi helps business buyers find suppliers in India, compare their full delivered price, and place orders. It helps suppliers receive requests that fit what they make. KaantaMandi is a platform. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, the contract of sale is between the buyer and the supplier, and payment goes directly between them.

4. Orders, delivery and payment

Nothing is ordered until the buyer approves it, in the app or by replying on WhatsApp. Repeat orders only prepare a fresh request; they never order by themselves. In a buying team, orders above a member's limit go to the account owner first. An approved order is a purchase order to the supplier at the price shown. Suppliers record dispatch with the transporter and LR number; buyers confirm what arrived and can rate the supplier. On-time rates and ratings come only from these records. Payments recorded in the app are a record between buyer and supplier; online payments through Razorpay are processed by Razorpay. Quality, delivery, invoices, returns and payment are between buyer and supplier; we'll help when something goes wrong, but we're not responsible for the other party's obligations.
