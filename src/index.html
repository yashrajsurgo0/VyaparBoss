How the numbers are built

  • Landed cost = supplier price at the matching quantity tier + GST + estimated road freight.
  • Freight: part load at ₹2.50/kg plus ₹0.006/kg per km, or ₹9,000 + ₹38/km per 9-tonne truck when cheaper. Minimum ₹1,200.
  • Road distance = straight-line distance × 1.3. Transit = 1 day per 450 km.
  • Ranking: 60% landed cost, 25% on-time rate, 15% buyer rating. Suppliers that miss the deadline are listed separately.
  • Savings compare the chosen quote with the average of all eligible quotes for that RFQ.
  • Take rate of 1.5% on goods value is a placeholder until the pilot sets real pricing.
