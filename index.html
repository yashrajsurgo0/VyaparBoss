How these numbers are worked out
  • Delivered cost is the supplier's price for your quantity, plus GST, plus estimated road freight.
  • Freight: part load ₹2.50/kg plus ₹0.006/kg per km, or ₹9,000 + ₹38/km per 9-tonne truck when that's cheaper. Minimum ₹1,200.
  • Road distance is straight-line distance × 1.3, and trucks cover about 450 km a day.
  • Bhai's pick weighs delivered cost 60%, on-time record 25% and buyer rating 15%. Suppliers who can't meet your date are shown separately.
  • Savings compare the chosen quote with the average of all quotes for that request.
  • Platform revenue assumes a 1.5% fee on goods value until the pilot sets real pricing.
